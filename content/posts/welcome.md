Many have lamented the state of the internet in 2026. Large companies silo all of the content and we're largely subject to their whims. Unlike the early days of the web when you would [stumble upon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StumbleUpon) content through aggregators and individuals who would just build and link to cool things they enjoyed, our tastes are mostly shaped by [the](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk) [worst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg) [among](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok)[^1] us in favor of keeping us addicted and beaming ads into our brains.

Alas, this is not what this post is about, at least not directly. As they say, be the change you want to see in the world. Much as [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_London) has been built on the top of a Roman city, so too has the modern internet been built on the old. The difference is that the plumbing from the old internet still works. Yes, believe it or not, you can still experience social media where the [algorithm is you.](https://mastodon.social/)[^2] and as it turns out, people who just want to host a fansite or post about nerdy things without an expectation of it making them a bunch (or any) money can still do so extremely easily, and freely. 

I don't want to get too far off into the weeds here, but this site is made with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), a simple static site generator. I push the files to github, and then the site is built and served on Cloudlare. This feels like cheating.
